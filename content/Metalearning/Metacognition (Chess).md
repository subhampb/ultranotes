
1. Seeing the moves before playing grants greater success. Especially forcing moves with almost no variation (Single line) are easily seen.
2. Usually Low gain moves like a move which requires a sacrifice a piece just win one pawn after all exchanges remains unseen. Probably brains values its own piece more than opponents.
3. Removal of defense isn't just for pieces protecting pieces but for pieces protecting desirable squares.
4. Quick move using intuition is less reliable than calculating all the moves through.
5. Always track all your pieces not just attacking pieces or pieces used for plan. Look for active pieces i.e piece that can cover attacking squares and make them part of plan.
6. Often missed tactic : Distraction. Because it needs to give pieces for free. More risky than sacrifice.
7. Combined tactics are difficult to spot. Such as distraction and pin together. So look for individual tactics and find relation between them.
8. Brain keeps underestimating low value pieces like pawns.
9. During exhaustion brain only focuses on simplification and loses all advantage doing that.
