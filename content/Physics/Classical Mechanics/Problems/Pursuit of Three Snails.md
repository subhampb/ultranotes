Three snails start at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side 60 cm. Each moves toward the next snail with speed 5 cm/min.

### Observation 1: Symmetry Preservation

The configuration remains an equilateral triangle for all time.

**Pattern:**  
When a dynamical system has rotational/permutation symmetry, check whether the symmetry is preserved during evolution.

### Observation 2: Track the Right Quantity

Instead of tracking coordinates, track the side length s(t).

### Observation 3: Relative Velocity Along a Constraint

**Pattern:**  
When asked about changing distances, project relative velocity onto the separation vector.

### Observation 4: Constant Angle ⇒ Logarithmic Spiral

The velocity vector makes a constant angle with the radius vector.
