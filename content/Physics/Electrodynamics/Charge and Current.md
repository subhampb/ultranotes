Total charge in a given region V:

$$Q=\int_V d^3x\,\rho(\mathbf{x,t})$$
$$\rho(\mathbf{x},t) = q\,\delta^{(3)}\!\big(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{r}(t)\big).$$
Current Density:
$$I=\int_S \mathbf{J}\cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

$$\mathbf{J}(\mathbf{x},t)
=
\rho(\mathbf{x},t)\,\mathbf{v}(t)
=
q\,\mathbf{v}(t)\,\delta^{(3)}\!\big(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{r}(t)\big).
$$
Electron moving in wire:

$$
\rho = nq,
$$

$$
\mathbf{J} = nq\,\mathbf{v},
$$

For a uniform current density perpendicular to a cross-sectional area $A$,

$$
I = J|A|
$$
