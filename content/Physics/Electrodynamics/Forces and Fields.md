A field is a dynamical quantity that takes a value at every point in space and time.
Field lines can never cross each other, however, they are created and destroyed by charges. Positive charges act as source and negative charge act as sinks.


$$
\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{x}, t), \quad \mathbf{B}(\mathbf{x}, t)
$$
Lorentz force law:

$$
\mathbf{F}
=
q\left(
\mathbf{E}
+
\dot{\mathbf{x}}
\times
\mathbf{B}
\right)
$$

In form of force density:

$$
\mathbf{f}
=
\rho\,\mathbf{E}
+
\mathbf{J}
\times
\mathbf{B}
$$
