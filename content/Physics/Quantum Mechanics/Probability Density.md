
The probability density for a particle to sit at point x at time t:
$$P(x,t)=\left|\psi(x,t)\right|^2$$
The probability that particle sits in some finite volume $V \in \mathbb{R}^3$ :
$$P_V=\int_V P(x,t)\,d^3x$$
Normalisation:
Normalised wavefunction:
$$\int d^3x\,P(x,t)
=
\int d^3x\,\left|\psi(x,t)\right|^2
=
1$$
Normalisable wavefunction:
$$\int d^3x\,\left|\psi(x,t)\right|^2
=
N
<
\infty$$
Wavefunction is normalisable only if it vanishes at spatial infinity:
$$\psi(x,t)\xrightarrow[|x|\to\infty]{}0$$
Constructing a normalised wavefunction from normalisable wavefunction:
$$\psi(x,t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\,\Psi(x,t)$$

$$\psi(x)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}
\left(
e^{-a(x-X_1)^2}
+
e^{-a(x-X_2)^2}
\right)$$
