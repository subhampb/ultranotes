
A particle sitting in a superposition of two well separated position $X_1$ and $X_2$ can evolve in two ways:

- If not observed, state evolves through the Schrödinger equation:
$$i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi(x,t)}{\partial t}
=
\hat{H}\psi(x,t)$$
- If observed, the wavefunction collapses:
$$\psi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\, e^{-a(x-X_1)^2}$$
