Classical Particles:

Particles pass through slit 1 and slit 2, 
If slit 1 is open only, distribution is $P_1(x)$.
If slit 2 is open only, distribution is $P_2(x)$.
If both slits are open, $P_{12}(x) = P_1(x) + P_2(x)$  (No interference occurs)

Classical Waves:

Each slit act as source of outgoing wave.
Waves from two slit combine and form: Bright fringes (constructive interference) & Dark fringes (destructive interference)

Quantum Electrons:

"In quantum world, the information is physical"

Electrons acts a wavefunction passing through both slits at once, creating interference pattern.
If you try to record information anyhow then, interference pattern disappears.
$$\psi_{12}(x) \to \psi_1(x)$$


One slit open: $P_1(x) = |\psi_1(x)|^2$,  $P_2(x) = |\psi_2(x)|^2$ 
Both slit open: $$P_{12}(x) = |\psi_1(x) + \psi_2(x)|^2, \qquad \psi_{12}(x) = \psi_1(x) + \psi_2(x)$$
$$= P_1(x) + P_2(x) + \left(\psi_1^*(x)\psi_2(x) + \psi_2^*(x)\psi_1(x)\right)
$$


$\left(\psi_1^*(x)\psi_2(x) + \psi_2^*(x)\psi_1(x)\right)$ is responsible for interference pattern.
