
Equation of motion of a particle with mass $m$ in one spatial dimension in the presence of potential $V(x)$:

$$m \ddot{x} = -\frac{dV}{dx}$$

In quantum mechanics we solve for:

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial x^2} + V(x)\Psi$$
