### Particle on a Circle

$$\psi_n(\theta) = e^{in\theta}$$

$$\psi_{-n}(\theta) = e^{-in\theta}$$

Here,

$$\vert{}\psi_n\vert{}^2 \neq \vert{}\psi_{-n}\vert{}^2$$

$n$ and $-n$ correspond to **different states**.

- **Momentum:**
    
    $$p = \hbar k$$
    
- **Wave Type:**
    
    **Travelling wave**
    

### Particle in an Infinite Potential Well

$$\psi_n(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{L}} \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$$

However,

$$\psi_{-n}(x) = -\psi_n(x)$$

$$\vert{}\psi_{-n}\vert{}^2 = \vert{}-\psi_n\vert{}^2 = \vert{}\psi_n\vert{}^2$$

$n$ and $-n$ **don't correspond to different states** (they represent the same physical state up to an overall phase factor/sign).

- **Momentum:**
    
    $$p = +\hbar k \quad \text{and} \quad p = -\hbar k$$
    
- **Wave Type:**
    
    **Standing wave**
